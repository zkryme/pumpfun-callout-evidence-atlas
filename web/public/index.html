04 · COVERAGE & METHOD

Scope beside the conclusion

Methodology and boundaries

Calls and early trades come from Pump.fun. Funding and linked-wallet event ledgers use parsed Helius transfers/transactions. “No caller-to-founder link found” is limited to this 97-call snapshot, the retained first-in funding paths (up to three hops), and the 30-day pre-launch review. Broad service/exchange-like funders are not treated as common ownership. Root-funder and fee fields are retained as unavailable in raw data but hidden from normal tables.
