06 Method & boundaries

What the evidence can—and cannot—say

01

Collect calls

Normalize 97 profile activity records and resolve each token’s mint, creator, launch time, and call time.

02

Read early trades

Capture the first 50 buyers per token, measuring seconds from launch and seconds relative to the call.

03

Detect patterns

Score same-transaction, same-slot, near-identical-size, supply concentration, and repeat-buyer signals.

04

Separate proof

Report timing clusters separately from corroborated funding or ownership evidence. Unknown evidence remains unknown.
